Key aspects of the Tron: Legacy color scheme include:

Primary Palette: Dark, desaturated blues/teals represent the grid, while glowing neon orange and light blue are used for character suits and light cycles to create visual conflict.

Character Colors:
Blue: Sam Flynn and other protagonists/neutral programs.
Orange/Yellow: Clu 2 and his followers.
White: Users and Isos (Jeff Bridges/Quorra).
Red: Used for military programs, tanks, and, in some contexts, enemies.
Purple: Data pushers (specialized, varied programs).

Atmosphere: The aesthetic is characterized by dark, metallic, and reflective environments, with neon lights providing the primary illumination.

Digital Color Hex Codes:
Dark Background: #0A0A0A
Deep Blue: #16222E, #274259
Light/Cyan Blue: #7EA6BF, 24-202-230
Orange: #FF9F1C (approximate)
White: #F9FDFC 

For digital design or UI projects, this theme is often replicated using high-contrast themes that prioritize neon, glowing effects against a near-black background.
