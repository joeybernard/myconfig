data collection and preparation are the first steps, and arguably the most important

Data collection - the systematic and well-organized process of gathering and accurately conveying information and aspects related to a specific phenomenon or event
- data collection methods
	- experiments
	- surveys
	- observations
	- focus groups
	- interviews
	- document analysis
- observational data - often used in qualitative research, observing and recording behaviour
- transactional data - related to transactions or interactions between individuals, businesses or systems - more often in quantitative research
