At the National Health Data Summit, they discussed trying to use standardized language. One of the topics was levels of data sensitivity and access:
- open - there are no restrictions on data access
- registered - access is managed, users must have a registered account
- controlled - access is fully controlled, data probably has physical requirements along with digital requirements
