## Army of Darkness: Hellbelies & Deadnecks

### Description

The next thrilling chapter in the life of "Chosen One" Ashley J. Williams beings here! Opening with a return to the past, Ash has reluctantly embraced his role of humanity's protector and fully taken on the job of guarding the world against the evil powers of the Necronomicon. What could go wrong? Well, just about everything... Featuring issues 13-17 of DYNAMITE ENTERTAINMENT's fan favorite comic books series, HELLBILLIES and DEADNECKS features the writing team of James Kuhoric and Mike Raicht, along with artist Scott Cohn! Also included in this trade paperback collection is a complete cover gallery, featuring the art of Stjepan (The Darkness, Witchblade) Sejic and Fabiano (ARMY OF DARKNESS/MARVEL ZOMBIES) Neves!
