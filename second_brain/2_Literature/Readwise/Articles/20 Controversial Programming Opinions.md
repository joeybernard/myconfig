## Metadata
- Author: [[Yannis Rizos]]
- Full Title: 20 Controversial Programming Opinions
- Category: #articles
- Summary: The article presents 20 controversial programming opinions, including the idea that programmers who don't code in their spare time for fun will never become as good as those who do, that unit testing won't help you write good code, and that readability is the most important aspect of code. Other opinions include the belief that public fields are not necessarily evil, that XML should only be used when necessary, and that software development is just a job. The article also includes opinions on the importance of being able to write code and the limitations of design patterns.
