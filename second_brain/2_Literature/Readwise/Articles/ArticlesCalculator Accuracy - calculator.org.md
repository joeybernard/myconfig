## Highlights
- One of the bugbears of developing calculator emulators is that the floating point arithmetic on a computer is actually fundamentally different to that in a pocket calculator. This is due to the general purpose nature of computers (and to some extent the vagaries of the IEEE floating point standard), and the very application specific way that calculator chips are designed. So if you rely on the computer's arithmetic functions (which nowadays are almost invariably implemented in hardware) you can get some nasty surprises.
