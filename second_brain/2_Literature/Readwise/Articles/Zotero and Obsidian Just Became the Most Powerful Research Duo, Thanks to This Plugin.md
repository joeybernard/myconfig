## Highlights
- Everything that's in my Zotero library now lives inside Obsidian, and better yet, it automatically updates when I add new content to it. Every reference, whether it's a website, a book, or a scientific paper, is readily accessible. I can click the link inside the library to view the original source, all without ever leaving Obsidian. That means no more bouncing around between apps or browser windows; it streamlines my workflow and helps me stay focused at times when it would be easy to get distracted. - On top of that, the plugin isn't yet officially part of Obsidian's plugin library. I installed it through BRAT, but until it has been vetted and added to the community list, it requires more work to install than other plugins. There are simpler alternatives, and if you're just using Zotero on a casual basis, you probably don't need to go to this much trouble.
