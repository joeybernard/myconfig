- Summary: Pathological demand avoidance (PDA) can make everyday tasks feel overwhelming, especially for those with ADHD or autism. To cope, it's important to understand personal triggers, reframe demands, and find supportive strategies that work for you. Embracing your own way of doing things and allowing for breaks can help manage anxiety and increase motivation.
