This are general notes on how CEL works.

- Destiny is used for the non-credit courses offered through CEL
- Destiny came into full use around April 2023
- Every 15 minutes, Destiny prepares a job file to update D2L
- Usernames within Destiny are the fully qualified email address given during registration
- Students can't access lms.ub.ca directly. They must go through Destiny, where they are tunneled through to D2L.
- IDs from Destiny are of the form X.....
- CEL is currently working with ITS to have a proper UNB account created for these students. This may happen over the summer.
- Any issues with students should be sent to customerservice@unb.ca
